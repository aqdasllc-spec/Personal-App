# Folio — what's inside and where to find it

Four tabs along the bottom (a side rail on tablets and desktop): **Home, Library, Planner, Settings**.
Everything works offline. Signing in only adds syncing between your devices.

---

## Home
- **Quran (the gold cabinet at the top):** tap the Quran in its glass niche: the lamp lights up and the Quran opens. Next to it, **Daily Ayah** and **Daily Hadith** (a new one each day), in **اردو** or **English** (the switch at the top right; remembered). Tap the text to read it in full, with the Arabic, **Listen** (ayah), **Open in Quran** and **Copy**.
- **Prayer reminders** (Prayer times page → Reminders): titled *Salat-ul-Fajr · نمازِ فجر* (Jumu'ah on Fridays). Choose the sound (the phone's notification sound, alarm sound, ringtone, or vibrate only). A **second reminder** "get up and pray" comes the minutes and seconds you choose after the prayer time, for Fajr only or every prayer with a bell, with its own sound, and can **keep ringing until you open it**. *Try it* shows each reminder in 5 seconds.
- **Prayer times (the line at the foot of the cabinet):** the next prayer with a countdown and today's Hijri date. Tap it for all of today's times, a bell per prayer (a reminder at that time), your place (from the phone, or typed in), the calculation method (Karachi by default), Asr (Hanafi by default) and a ±2 day Hijri adjustment for local moon sighting. Calculated on the phone; nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Khatm goal:** in the Quran, *Set a khatm goal* (7 to 60 days, from Al-Fatiha or from where you are): each day's pages, on track or how many pages behind, progress; it follows your reading by itself. Home shows today's pages under the Quran.
- **In the Quran:** every surah (search by name or number; spellings like "Yasin" or "Fatiha" work) or the 30 juz; Continue reading. Each ayah: Arabic (Uthmani script) with the Urdu (Fateh Muhammad Jalandhry), English (Saheeh International), both, or Arabic only (the translate button). **Listen** recites from any ayah with a choice of 14 reciters, optionally followed by each ayah's Urdu or English translation, and keeps going with the screen off (lock-screen controls); the ayah being recited is highlighted and kept on screen. Text size and reciter: the sliders button. Your place is remembered as you read (or tap the pin on an ayah).
  - **Search** (magnifier): a word in Arabic (any spelling, with or without vowel marks; Arabic script also searches the Urdu) or English.
  - **Bookmarks** with notes (bookmark button on an ayah; the Bookmarks tab lists them).
  - **Share** an ayah as a picture (gold and green card, with Urdu, English or both) or copy the text.
  - **Memorise (Hifz)** (cap button in a surah): choose ayahs, repeat each 1-10×, repeat the selection, a pause to say it back, and *Hide the Arabic* (first word as a cue, tap to show).
  - **Listen without internet** (download button in a surah): this surah or the whole Quran for the chosen reciter (about 0.8-2.4 GB depending on the reciter's quality); played from the phone by itself; stop, continue, delete.
  - **Ayah of the day each morning** (Quran settings): a notification at the time you choose. Everything except the recitation works offline; recitations stream from the internet.
- **Reading** card: pages read today against your daily goal (tap *Goal* to change it), your streak, the last 7 days and time with a book open. A page counts after a few seconds on it, once per day.
- **Today** at a glance: tasks due, events, a one-line add box ("Add a task for today…").
- **Continue reading**: books you have started. The small menu switches between *Recently read first* and *My order* (the Library's order).
- Tap a book for a preview card; tap again to read.

## Library
- **Add PDFs** (the + button). Same file twice is detected.
- **Shelf style** (Settings → Library): *Classic* (rows of spines), *Turning books* (tap a spine and the book turns to show its cover, with its progress in a card at the bottom; tap the cover or Read to open it, Put back to return it) or *Glass cabinets* (the Library opens on a wall of small glass cabinets, one per shelf plus *Unsorted*; tap one and its doors swing open onto two rows of books that turn the same way; the phone's Back closes the cabinet). While you search, filter, show favourites or rearrange, Glass cabinets shows the books on open shelves. *Cover carousel*: the covers stand in a 3D row; swipe, use the arrows or tap a side cover to bring it to the middle; the middle one shows its progress with More and Read. The **full-screen** button (top right) shows the covers as big as the screen allows, on a soft wash of the book's colour, with when you last read it; Close (or Back) returns to the shelf at the same book.
- **Spotlight search** (Settings → Library, on by default): on the shelf, searching keeps every book in place, greys out the others and lights up the matches with a gold glow, and scrolls to the first one. Turn it off to have the search hide the other books instead (Grid, List and the carousel always do that).
- **Ribbons and dust** (Settings → Library, on by default): a red ribbon runs down a spine as far as you have read; finished books get gold bands; books you have not opened for about two months start to gather dust (more the longer they wait).
- **Shelves**: group books; the pen next to a shelf = *edit shelf* (books wiggle; drag them left/right to reorder).
- **Tag colours**: Sort and filter → Tags → Manage → tap the circle by a tag to give it one of 8 colours; a coloured dot then shows on the filter chips and the book preview.
- **Sort and filter** (recent, title, author, status, tags) and **Saved views**: pick a status and tags, tap *Save this view*, give it a name; tap the view later to bring those filters back (long-lived, up to 20).
- **Search inside all books** (magnifier-with-list button): type a word, see every place it appears in your books (scans too, once made searchable); tap a hit to open the book at that page with the search ready. Its **Highlights** tab shows every highlight you made, filtered by colour; press and hold a colour to say what it means to you (for example yellow = Important).
- **Add a whole folder** (folder button): choose a folder and every PDF in it and its sub-folders is added after you confirm.
- **Search**,  **Select several books** (checklist icon) to tag, move, favourite or remove many at once.
- **Long-press / hover** a book for a preview (first page, progress, tags).
- Cloud dot on a book: red = only on this device, green = also in your cloud (Sync / Unsync).
- Removed books wait **30 days** in Settings → Storage → Recently removed.

## Reading (open a book)
**Top bar:** title with the page number, **Search**, **Aa** (reading look), **⋮** (more).
**Bottom:** the page slider, then five tools: **Contents** (bookmarks and highlights), **Pages** (a grid of page pictures: tap one to jump; bookmarked and drawn pages are marked), **Bookmark**, **Draw**, **Listen**.

| You want to… | Do this |
|---|---|
| Turn the page | Tap the left/right edge of the page, or swipe/scroll |
| Hide the bars (immersive) | Tap the middle of the page |
| Jump anywhere | Drag the page slider |
| Change look | **Aa**: colour scheme (Original, Paper, Sepia, Night, Black), Dim, Warm light, sideways paging, orientation lock, keep screen on |
| Search the text | **Search** (magnifier) |
| Bookmark | **Bookmark** tool; highlights: select text → Highlight; **Contents** lists both, with notes and export |
| Draw or write on a page | **Draw**: the bottom bars give way to one slim pen bar. One finger (or a stylus) draws; **two fingers pinch-zoom** the page. The round button opens colour, thickness and **Pen / Highlighter**; then Eraser (touch a stroke), Undo, Clear page and Done. A stylus wins over a resting palm. **Stylus only** (in the round button's pop-up) makes fingers scroll and zoom normally while only the stylus draws. One finger does not scroll while the pen is on: use two fingers, or Done |
| Look up a word | Select text → **Look up** → Meaning (dictionary search) or Translate (into your phone's language); both open your browser and send the selected text to that website |
| Share your drawings | ⋮ menu → **Export as PDF with my drawings**: this page, pages with drawings, or the whole book (up to 60 pages). The new PDF has the pages as pictures with your drawing on top; the original is not changed |
| Have it read aloud | **Listen**: reads from this page and turns pages by itself. Pause/Play, speed (0.75x to 2x), close. Uses your phone's voice (by default it picks an installed voice that matches the language of the page being read; the people icon in the voice bar lets you turn that off and choose the language and voice yourself), and keeps reading with the screen off, with Play/Pause, Previous/Next page and Stop on the lock screen and in the notification shade. Scans are read from the recognised text; a scan that is not made searchable yet says so |
| Scanned or handwritten PDF | See below |

### Scanned and handwritten PDFs
- Folio checks each book the first time you open it. If it is a **scan** (pictures of pages) a blue banner offers **Make searchable**.
- It then reads the pages on your phone (offline, a page at a time). You can **Stop** and carry on later. Searching then finds words in scans and marks the line on the page.
- Any book: ⋮ menu → **Make searchable (scans, handwriting)**; **Text of this page** shows and copies what was read; **Read the pages again** redoes it.
- Honest limits: works for printed text in Latin script (English and most European languages). **Handwriting is often not recognised**, and faint or skewed scans give weaker results. Phones and tablets only.

## Planner
- **Day / Week / Month** views. Quick-add at the top of the Day view.
- Tasks have notes, due date/time, repeat, reminders, **sub-tasks** (drag to reorder).
- Drag a task onto a day (Month/Week) or a time slot (Day) to move it.
- Events live on the same timeline.

## Settings
- **Account & sync**, **App lock** (fingerprint / PIN), **Rotation**. Light and dark follow your phone.
- **Storage**: space used, recently removed books.
- **Sync**: with an account, tasks and events sync as before, and now also your **highlights, bookmarks, drawings, tag colours and saved views** (the PDFs themselves only when you press Sync on a book).
- **Backup**: save everything (books, notes, highlights, drawings, tag colours, scanned-page text) to a file; restore it later.
- **Help**: replay the welcome tour, gesture cheat-sheet.

## App-icon shortcuts (Android)
Press and hold the Folio icon: **Continue reading**, **Add a task**, **Planner: today**, **Library**.
